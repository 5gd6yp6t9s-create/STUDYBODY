PARTICIPANT INFORMATION & CONSENT

Before using STUDYBODY

This prototype is designed for research self-monitoring of study-related physical discomfort. It is not a diagnostic or treatment system.

What participation involves

Day 0 baseline, Study Session logs for up to 28 days, body-map check-ins, and a Day 28 exit questionnaire.

Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. A real study deployment must provide the approved withdrawal procedure and researcher contact information.

Data collected

Study context, session timing, breaks, stress/focus ratings, self-reported discomfort, and optional exploratory jaw/temple items. Do not enter names, email addresses, phone numbers, or student IDs into free-text fields.

Privacy

This v0.5 demo stores data locally in the browser. A real multi-participant study requires a secure backend and approved data-management procedures.

Local prototype tools for pilot preparation. No participant-facing diagnostic interpretation is generated.

Participant registry

v0.5 introduces a local multi-participant architecture for technical testing. Each participant has an isolated record bundle under a pseudonymous ID.

Study ID validation

Study ID is generated automatically for participants. Nicknames are for on-screen identification only and are excluded from the analysis-ready dataset.

Dataset management

Import/export bundles are for prototype testing. Import validates schema and participant IDs before adding records.

Analysis-ready export
